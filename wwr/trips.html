Our Rafting Trips

Find Your Perfect Adventure

Discover the perfect rafting adventure for every experience level, from relaxing beginner trips to exciting advanced rapids.

Beginner Half-Day Trip

This trip is perfect for families, beginners, and anyone who wants to enjoy a relaxing rafting adventure. Paddle through calm waters while learning basic rafting skills with an experienced guide.

Intermediate Full-Day Trip

Ready for a bigger adventure? This full-day trip combines peaceful river sections with exciting rapids. Participants will practice their paddling skills, work together to navigate obstacles, and enjoy the surrounding landscape. The trip includes breaks to rest, enjoy lunch, and appreciate the natural beauty of the river.

Advanced Rapids Adventure

Designed for experienced rafters seeking an adrenaline-filled challenge, this trip features powerful rapids, technical passages, and complex maneuvers. Participants must be confident swimmers, comfortable with demanding river conditions, and capable of following a guide's instructions quickly.
