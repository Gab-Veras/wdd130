Wild Water Rafting

Our mission is simple: to give every guest a safe, thrilling, and unforgettable day on the river. Whether it is your first paddle stroke or your hundredth rapid, our guides will make sure you leave with a smile.

History

Wild Water Rafting began in 2005 when a small group of river guides pooled their savings to buy two rafts and a used van. What started as weekend trips for friends quickly grew into a family business. Today our certified guides lead thousands of adventurers down the river every season, but we still treat every guest like one of our first.
